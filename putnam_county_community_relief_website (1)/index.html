WHY WE EXIST

Turn community concern into measurable action.

Poverty and financial hardship affect real families, but statistics only tell part of the story. Our goal is to understand local needs, learn from organizations already doing this work, identify gaps, and then use student leadership to help fill them.

Direct ReliefResearchPartnershipsAwareness

THE RESEARCH

Understand the need before trying to solve it.

Our research team will combine public data with community input and conversations with local organizations.

01

Poverty

Track poverty rates, household income, child poverty, and other indicators using verified public sources.

02

Employment

Study unemployment, labor force participation, major industries, and potential barriers to employment.

03

Community Need

Look at food access, transportation, housing, and where existing resources may not reach enough people.

04

Hidden Need

Recognize that surveys can miss people. Compare survey results with public data and local organizations' experience.

Important: Statistics displayed publicly will be verified before publication and linked to their original sources.
